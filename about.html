Teen Lifestyle Medicine is a youth-led nonprofit. We turn the science of healthy living into clubs, events, and content made by teens, for teens — and the grown-ups who back them.

Why we exist

Up to 80% of chronic disease traces back to how we live. The habits behind it form early — so the smartest place to start is adolescence, and the most convincing messenger to a teen is another teen.

How we started

A group chat that
got out of hand.

NextGen LIFE began in 2025 when a high-schooler noticed that articles about sleep, food, and stress were not written for people their age.

So we made our own. Today, chapters run cook-alongs, challenges, and wellbeing workshops, all led by students.

We stay close to the evidence, guided by mentors in lifestyle medicine.
